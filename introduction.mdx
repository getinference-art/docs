When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overview or Google AI Mode questions about businesses like yours, does your brand show up in the answer?

Which of these engines you track depends on your plan. The pricing page lists the engines included with each one.

The platform helps you find out, and gives you the tools to improve.

## What the Platform Does

The platform monitors how AI search engines talk about your brand. Each tracking run sends your real search prompts to the major AI platforms and checks:

- **Does the AI mention your brand by name?** When someone asks for recommendations in your category, is your business in the answer?
- **Does it link to your website?** AI answers often include citations, and the platform detects whether yours is among them.
- **Does it show you on the map?** Google AI Mode and AI Overviews sometimes display a map with places. We check if your business appears there.
- **How do you compare to competitors?** Your Share of Voice score tells you what percentage of AI answers mention your brand, and the briefing ranks the competitors that came up most often alongside you.

Based on all of this, the platform gives you clear, actionable recommendations to strengthen your presence in AI-generated answers.

<Note>
Tracking runs are not automatic or daily. Each plan includes an allowance of tracking
runs per billing month and you decide when to spend them. AI agent visits to your own
site are different: those are recorded continuously by the crawler analytics script.
</Note>
